Indian banks overseas are running FCNR(B) campaigns that promise up to **14%** on your idle dollars. The headline is real, but it depends on leverage, and leverage cuts both ways.

## How the 9x leverage works

- You put in **$1M** and earn the fixed FD rate of around **5.5–6%**.
- The bank also lends you **$9M at SOFR plus a margin**, which you deposit alongside your own money.
- You now earn 5.5–6% on the full **$10M**, while paying SOFR+ only on the $9M you borrowed.

The gap between the deposit rate and your borrowing cost, earned nine times over on borrowed money, is what lifts the return on your own $1M towards the headline figure.

## The catch: SOFR

The deposit rate is fixed. Your borrowing cost is not.

- **If SOFR drifts down,** the spread widens and the deal looks great.
- **If SOFR goes up,** the spread narrows, and on nine times leverage, it starts to hurt quickly.

## Not just for individuals

This is not only an NRI product. Corporates in the UAE have been running leveraged deposits like this for years, arranged through their wealth-management banks. The mechanics are the same; only the amounts are bigger.
