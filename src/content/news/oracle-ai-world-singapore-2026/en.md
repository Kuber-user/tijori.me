On 14 April 2026, the Tijori team was at Oracle AI World Tour Singapore at Marina Bay Sands, exploring the future of treasury transformation for Oracle customers.

## The treasury gap in Oracle ERP

Our founder, Shyam Arunachalam, spoke about a critical blind spot for Oracle customers: treasury. Oracle Financials runs the books, but much of the treasury work around it, such as bank facilities, borrowings, investments, guarantees and daily cash positions, still lives in spreadsheets and bank portals.

## How Tijori fills it

We demonstrated how Tijori, our treasury management system built on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), complements Oracle Financials and bridges that gap with pre-built integrations. The aim: give treasury teams control, visibility and a clear return on investment, without leaving the Oracle ecosystem.
