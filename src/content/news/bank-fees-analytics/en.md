Most groups that bank with several banks have never seen their bank charges side by side. Two simple questions usually go unanswered:

1. **What are different banks charging your company for identical services?**
2. **At what ticket size does the cheapest bank stop being the cheapest?**

The second question matters more than it looks. Fee structures differ: one bank may be cheapest for small transfers and expensive for large ones, another the other way round. Picking "the cheapest bank" without looking at ticket size can cost more than it saves.
