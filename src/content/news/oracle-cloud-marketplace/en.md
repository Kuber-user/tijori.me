Tijori Treasury Management System is now listed on **[Oracle Cloud Marketplace](https://marketplace.oracle.com/app/tijoritms)**. Oracle customers can discover Tijori directly from Oracle's own marketplace.

## What Tijori brings to Oracle customers

Tijori brings together the core pillars of modern treasury in one platform:

- Cash and liquidity
- Debt and borrowings
- Investments
- FX and risk
- Payments and settlements
- Trade finance
- AI-powered treasury insights

## Why it matters

For us, this is more than a marketplace listing. It is another step towards a treasury management system that sits naturally within the enterprise technology stack, connecting treasury teams, banks and ERPs, and giving CFOs and treasurers better visibility and control.

Tijori runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and integrates with Oracle Fusion and Oracle EBS. Read how we presented this at [Oracle AI World Tour Singapore](/en/news/oracle-ai-world-singapore-2026), or [book a 30-minute demo](/en/company/contact).
