Seertree, the company behind Tijori, has entered a strategic partnership with **DiXiO**, an official SWIFT platform partner and cloud provider of financial messaging. DiXiO serves banks, financial institutions, large corporates and fintechs in more than 60 countries.

The partnership was announced in January 2025, when Tijori was still called Kuber TMS.

## Why it matters

Connecting a treasury system to each bank usually means a separate Host-to-Host (H2H) project per bank, with its own file formats, testing and long timelines. Through DiXiO, Tijori reaches banks over the SWIFT network instead:

- **One connection, many banks.** DiXiO hosts the customer's BIC and acts as the gateway to SWIFT, with more than 10,000 banks reachable.
- **Statements and balances flow in automatically**, giving treasury teams an up-to-date view of balances across all their banks.
- **Payments go out from Tijori.** Domestic and cross-border vendor payments are sent through DiXiO's messaging platform, with visibility of fees and remittance timelines, automatic status updates and reconciliation back to the ERP.

## How it works

Tijori talks to DiXiO's Smart Messaging Platform through an API or sFTP. The platform handles the message formats banks expect, from SWIFT MT and ISO 20022 MX to JSON, TXT and CSV, and sends them over SWIFT. For the treasury team, it simply means bank data arrives in Tijori and payments leave it, without managing a separate connection per bank.

## What it means for treasury teams

With bank data arriving on its own, Tijori can keep cash positions current and build cash forecasts on actual balances, so investment and borrowing decisions are based on today's numbers rather than yesterday's spreadsheet.
