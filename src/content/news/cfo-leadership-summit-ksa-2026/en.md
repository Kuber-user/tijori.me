On 7 October 2026, Tijori joined finance leaders from across Saudi Arabia at the 26th edition of the CFO Leadership Summit, held at the Riyadh Marriott Hotel. Tijori took part as a **Silver Partner**, with its own booth on the exhibition floor.

## A busy day at booth R9

Our founder, Shyam Arunachalam, described the footfall at the Tijori booth as overwhelming. Through the day we spoke with CFOs and treasury teams from some of the Kingdom's leading enterprises about the problems they face every day:

- Getting one up-to-date view of cash across many banks and entities
- Keeping track of bank facilities, guarantees and letters of credit
- Connecting treasury to their ERP without a long, heavy implementation

## What we showed

We introduced Tijori as a single platform for enterprise treasury teams, built around the way finance teams in the Kingdom actually work. Bank facilities, guarantees and letters of credit are part of its core, not added later. It brings cash, debt, investments, FX, payments and trade finance into one view, connected to banks and to ERPs such as Oracle Fusion and SAP.
