Seertree Global Services, the company behind Tijori, has moved into a new office at **WorkEZ Park Centre, Alwarpet, Chennai**.

The new space was designed for the way our team works: creative corners that spark conversations and collaborative workspaces that bring people together. In the words of our founder, Shyam Arunachalam, it has "the vibes that make you want to ditch WFH and come out and work here", helped along by the Lavazza coffee.

Chennai is one of our three offices, alongside Dubai (DIFC) and Abu Dhabi. New space, fresh energy, same team.
