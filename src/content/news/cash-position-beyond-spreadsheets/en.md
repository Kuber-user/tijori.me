Tijori's cash position goes beyond a balance summary. It computes the **estimated closing balance for each bank account**, and gives AI-led recommendations on the options available to deal with a cash **surplus or deficit**.

Because Tijori is also built for bank facilities, debt, trade finance and investments, with open banking connectivity and localisation for the region, the cash position sits next to everything that moves it.
