We have reworked Tijori's FX exposure view at least six times. We think we have finally landed on the sharp, clutter-free dashboard that dealers actually want.

## Built for the people running the FX book

An FX dashboard can easily show everything and help with nothing. Each round of redesign removed something rather than adding it, until what was left answered the questions a dealer asks before deciding whether to hedge:

- **Timeline:** when do our exposures fall due?
- **Context:** how big is each exposure, and where does it come from?
- **Direction:** which way is it moving, and does it need action?

## Fresh data, straight from the ERP

The data refreshes **every 15 minutes, directly from your ERP**, so the view reflects what is booked now rather than last month's extract.
